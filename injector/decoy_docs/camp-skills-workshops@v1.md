Our stores run free workshops on skills such as map reading, fire safety and cooking on a camp stove. Sessions last about 90 minutes and are held on weekday evenings.

Workshops are limited to 15 people, so reserve a spot at the store. Bring your own gear or borrow ours for the evening.
