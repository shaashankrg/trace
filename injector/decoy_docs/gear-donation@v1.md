title: Gear Donation Program
published_at: 2026-10-01
---
Drop off clean, usable outdoor gear at any Kestrel store and we pass it on to regional youth outdoor programs. Donated items are checked and cleaned by volunteers before they are handed out.

We cannot accept damaged tents, expired climbing ropes or used safety equipment such as helmets and harnesses.
