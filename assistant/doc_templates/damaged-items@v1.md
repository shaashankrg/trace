If an item arrives damaged, report it within {{damaged_report_days}} days of delivery. Email {{support_email}} with your order number and photos of the damage and the packaging.

We ship a replacement at no cost, or refund the item in full if a replacement is not available. You do not need to return the damaged item unless we ask for it.
