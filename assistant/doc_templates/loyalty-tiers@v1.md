Trail Rewards has three tiers based on spending in a calendar year. Base members earn points at the standard rate.

Silver members, who spend {{silver_tier_spend}} or more in a year, earn {{silver_points_multiplier}} times the standard points. Gold members, who spend {{gold_tier_spend}} or more, earn {{gold_points_multiplier}} times the standard points and get free express shipping on every order.

Tier status lasts through the end of the following calendar year.
