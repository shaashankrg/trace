If tracking shows your package as delivered but you cannot find it, check with neighbors and around your property. If the package still has not turned up {{lost_package_wait_days}} business days after the expected delivery date, contact support.

Lost package claims must be filed within {{lost_package_claim_days}} days of the expected delivery date. We send a replacement or a full refund once the claim is approved.
