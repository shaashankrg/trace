You can check out as a guest, but a Kestrel account lets you track orders, start returns, save addresses, and earn loyalty points.

To change the email on your account or close your account, contact support from the email address currently on file. Closing an account does not cancel orders that have already shipped, and any unused loyalty points are forfeited.
