Bring used Kestrel-brand gear to any store and get store credit of up to {{trade_in_max_pct}}% of the original price, depending on condition. Items must be clean and functional.

Traded-in gear is cleaned, repaired and resold, or recycled if it cannot be repaired. Trade-in credit cannot be paid out in cash.
