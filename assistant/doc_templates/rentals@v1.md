Kestrel stores rent tents, sleeping bags, backpacks, kayaks and snowshoes. Rentals require a refundable deposit of {{rental_deposit}} per item, returned when the gear comes back in good condition.

You can rent gear for up to {{rental_max_days}} days. Late returns are charged {{rental_late_fee}} per item per day. Rentals are available in stores only, not online.
