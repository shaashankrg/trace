If you find a lower price on an identical item from an authorized US retailer within {{price_match_window_days}} days of delivery, we refund the difference. The item must be in stock at the other retailer.

Price matching does not apply to clearance, marketplace sellers, membership-only prices, or bundles. Contact support with a link to the lower price to request a match.
