Kestrel Trail Rewards is free to join. Members earn {{loyalty_points_per_dollar}} point for every dollar spent, before tax and shipping.

Every {{loyalty_reward_points}} points converts to a {{loyalty_reward_value}} reward, issued automatically at the start of each month. Rewards expire {{loyalty_reward_expiry_days}} days after they are issued. Points expire after {{loyalty_points_expiry_months}} months without a purchase.

Points are removed when an order is returned or refunded. Rewards cannot be exchanged for cash.
