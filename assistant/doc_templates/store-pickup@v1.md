Choose store pickup at checkout to collect your order at any Kestrel store for free. We email you when the order is ready, usually within two hours during store hours.

We hold pickup orders for {{pickup_hold_days}} days. Orders not collected by then are cancelled and refunded. Bring a photo ID and your order number.
