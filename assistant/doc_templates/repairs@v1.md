Repairs covered by the warranty are free. For gear outside the warranty, we charge a {{repair_assessment_fee}} assessment fee, which is credited toward the cost of the repair if you go ahead.

Most repairs are completed within {{repair_turnaround_days}} business days. Drop gear off at any Kestrel store or mail it in with a repair form from your account page.
