Kestrel Trail Rewards is free to join. Members earn 1 point for every dollar spent, before tax and shipping.

Every 100 points converts to a $5 reward, issued automatically at the start of each month. Rewards expire 90 days after they are issued. Points expire after 12 months without a purchase.

Points are removed when an order is returned or refunded. Rewards cannot be exchanged for cash or applied to gift card purchases.
