Standard shipping is free on orders over $75 within the continental United States and takes 3 to 7 business days. Orders under $75 ship for a flat $6.95.

Orders placed before 1 p.m. Mountain Time ship the same business day. We also ship to Alaska and Hawaii for an extra fee shown at checkout.
