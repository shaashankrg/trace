Repairs covered by the warranty are free. For gear outside the warranty, we charge a $25 assessment fee, which is credited toward the cost of the repair if you go ahead.

Most repairs are completed within 10 business days. Drop gear off at any Kestrel store or mail it in with a repair form from your account page.
