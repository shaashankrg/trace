Kestrel-brand gear carries a 2-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, starting from the delivery date. Third-party brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty.

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or accidents, or unauthorized repairs.

To file a claim, email warranty@kestrel.example with your order number and photos of the defect. If the claim is approved we repair the item, replace it, or issue store credit for its purchase price.
