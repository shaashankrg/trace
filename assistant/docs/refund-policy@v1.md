You can return most Kestrel items, including boots, jackets, tents and packs, within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the item reaching our warehouse. Gift cards and personalized gear cannot be returned.
