You can exchange an unused item for a different size or color within 30 days of delivery. The exchange window is longer than the refund window, so an exchange is often possible after a refund is not.

Exchanges ship free. If the new item costs more, we charge the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference to your original payment method.
