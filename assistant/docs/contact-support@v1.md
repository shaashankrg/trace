You can reach Kestrel support by phone at 1-800-555-0142 or by email at help@kestrel.example. Phone support is open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Mountain Time, Monday through Saturday.

Email replies usually arrive within one business day. Have your order number ready to speed things up.
