You can check out as a guest, but a Kestrel account lets you track orders, start returns, and save addresses.

To reset your password, choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. We email a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If you do not receive it, check your spam folder or contact support.

To change the email on your account or close your account, contact support from the email address currently on file. Closing an account does not cancel orders that have already shipped.
