title: Caring for Your Gear
published_at: 2026-02-20
---
Wash waterproof jackets on a gentle cycle with a technical wash, then tumble dry on low to restore the water-repellent finish. Never use fabric softener.

Dry tents and sleeping bags fully before storing them, and store sleeping bags uncompressed. Clean leather boots with a damp brush and reapply conditioner every season.
