title: Trail Rewards Tiers
published_at: 2026-07-10
---
Trail Rewards has three tiers based on spending in a calendar year. Base members earn points at the standard rate.

Silver members, who spend $500 or more in a year, earn 1.25 times the standard points. Gold members, who spend $1,000 or more, earn 1.5 times the standard points and get free express shipping on every order.

Tier status lasts through the end of the following calendar year.
