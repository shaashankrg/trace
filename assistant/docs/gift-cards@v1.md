Kestrel gift cards are available in any amount from $10 up to $500. Gift cards never expire and have no fees.

Gift cards cannot be returned, refunded, or exchanged for cash, and they cannot be used to buy other gift cards. Lost or stolen gift cards can be replaced only if you have the original receipt.
