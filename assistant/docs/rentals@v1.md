Kestrel stores rent tents, sleeping bags, backpacks, kayaks and snowshoes. Rentals require a refundable deposit of $100 per item, returned when the gear comes back in good condition.

You can rent gear for up to 14 days. Late returns are charged $15 per item per day. Rentals are available in stores only, not online.
