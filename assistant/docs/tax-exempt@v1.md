title: Tax-Exempt Purchases
published_at: 2026-01-20
---
Tax-exempt organizations can apply for a tax-exempt account by sending their exemption certificate to support. We review certificates within 3 business days.

Once approved, sales tax is removed from orders placed while signed in to the approved account. Tax cannot be refunded on orders placed before approval.
