title: Cancelling an Order
published_at: 2026-02-01
---
You can cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it from the order page in your account. After that, the order is sent to the warehouse and cannot be cancelled.

If you miss the cancellation window, you can return the items after they arrive under the normal refund policy.
