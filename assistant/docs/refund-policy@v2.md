You can return most Kestrel items, including boots, jackets, tents and packs, within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include the receipt or order number.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the item reaching our warehouse.

Final-sale items, gift cards, and personalized gear cannot be returned. Items returned after 14 days are not eligible for a refund but may qualify for store credit at our discretion.
