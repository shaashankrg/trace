title: Lost or Missing Packages
published_at: 2026-03-20
---
If tracking shows your package as delivered but you cannot find it, check with neighbors and around your property. If the package still has not turned up 5 business days after the expected delivery date, contact support.

Lost package claims must be filed within 30 days of the expected delivery date. We send a replacement or a full refund once the claim is approved.
